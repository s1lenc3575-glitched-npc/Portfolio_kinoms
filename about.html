About Me

I am a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student with a strong interest in software development, information systems, and practical technology projects. My academic experience has allowed me to work with different areas of IT, including web development, database management, system analysis, and computer hardware. I approach development not only as a way of writing code, but as a process of understanding problems and creating solutions that can be applied in real-world situations.

Academic Background

My studies in Information Technology have provided me with a foundation in programming, database systems, networking, system development, and information management. Through academic projects and laboratory activities, I have gained experience in translating requirements into functional systems while considering usability, organization, and maintainability.

Coursework has also introduced me to software development methodologies, system analysis, database design, and documentation. These experiences have helped me understand that effective IT solutions require more than technical implementation. They also require proper planning, structured information, testing, and consideration of the people who will eventually use the system.

Hands-On Development

A significant part of my learning comes from hands-on development. I have worked on web-based projects involving HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL, including systems that require user interfaces, authentication, database operations, and structured information management. These projects have given me practical experience in connecting front-end interfaces with back-end processes and designing databases according to system requirements.

I also have experience working with system prototypes and academic case studies where the goal is to identify an existing problem and propose a technology-based solution. This involves gathering requirements, analyzing workflows, designing system features, organizing data, and developing prototypes that demonstrate how the proposed system can improve an existing process.

Rather than relying entirely on theoretical concepts, I prefer learning through implementation. Building, testing, encountering errors, and improving a system have become important parts of my development process.

Systems and Information Management

My interest in information systems extends beyond programming. I am particularly interested in how information can be collected, organized, stored, and presented to support daily operations and decision-making. Database design and information management have therefore become important areas of my academic work.

In system development projects, I consider how users interact with information and how different processes can be connected within one system. This includes identifying repetitive tasks, reducing manual record keeping, improving data accessibility, and designing features that can make operational workflows more organized and efficient.

Hardware and Embedded Projects

My interests also extend into hardware and embedded systems. I have worked with microcontrollers such as Arduino and ESP32 and explored projects involving sensors, displays, cameras, servos, audio components, and communication between hardware modules. These projects have strengthened my understanding of how software and hardware interact in practical applications.

Working with physical components has also taught me to approach technical problems differently. Issues involving power requirements, communication protocols, pin configurations, memory limitations, and component compatibility often require testing and troubleshooting rather than simply changing code. This hands-on experience has helped me develop a more practical approach to problem solving.

Approach to Technology

I believe that technology is most valuable when it addresses a specific need. Because of this, I try to understand the problem before deciding on a technical solution. Whether developing a web application, designing a database, or experimenting with an embedded system, I focus on understanding the requirements, testing the implementation, and improving the result based on actual observations.

I also recognize that technical skills require continuous development. New frameworks, tools, platforms, and hardware are constantly being introduced, which makes learning an ongoing part of working in IT. My current goal is to continue strengthening my programming and system development skills while gaining more experience with practical projects.

Looking Forward

As I continue my studies in Information Technology, I aim to build systems and applications that demonstrate both technical knowledge and practical understanding. I want my projects to show not only that I can write code, but also that I can analyze requirements, work with information, troubleshoot technical problems, and develop solutions with real users and operational needs in mind.

This portfolio represents part of that development. It documents my academic work, technical projects, experiments, and the skills I have gained through hands-on practice. Each project is an opportunity to apply what I have learned, identify what I still need to improve, and develop a stronger understanding of how technology can be used to solve practical problems.
